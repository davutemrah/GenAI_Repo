Why the estimate of c is biased when groups overlap

The Elkan–Noto estimate is the average naive score among seed members. Under SCAR that equals c × (average probability of being high value, among true high-value people). If true customers are unmistakable, that average is near 1 and the estimate is right. When groups overlap, many true customers look ambiguous, the average falls below 1, c is underestimated, and the audience size is overstated. The ranking (AUC) never changes, because dividing every score by the same number keeps their order.
